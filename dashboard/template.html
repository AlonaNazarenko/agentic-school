    Method & scores

    LightGBM model on a 5-min grid. Inputs at decision time τ use only information published before τ: each oblast's own alert history, neighbouring and upstream oblasts (lift graph learned on training data), attack-wave direction, raion-level alert shares and (if selected) official Air Force Telegram posts. Evaluated with 6 monthly walk-forward folds (Mar–Sep 2026); this page replays the test month of fold .
